EEG and EMG Neural Interface Software

Aug 2024 - May 2025 Neurotech Club

A year on the software side of the club, writing preprocessing and neural decoding pipelines for a full-body neural-controlled prosthetic.

I wrote the EEG and EMG preprocessing and neural decoding pipelines, and trained PyTorch models to classify the signals. The wider project was a full-body neural-controlled prosthetic for neurodegenerative disorders, built by a team that spanned several disciplines.

A substantial part of my time went to validating the data pipelines and confirming that results held across recording sessions rather than only on the session they were tuned on.
