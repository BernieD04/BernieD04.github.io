I'm a neural engineering student at the University of Illinois, part of the program's inaugural class and graduating in 2027, with a minor in computer science. My work spans the process of turning biological signals into useful information, from building the systems that record them to developing the software and machine learning models that interpret them.

My work has focused on brain-computer interfaces and neurotechnology, including decoding brain and muscle signals, predicting brain activity from electrical recordings, and building real-time systems that let people interact with computers without using their hands. Most of my work is in software, but I enjoy working across the whole process of turning a biological signal into something a computer can understand and act on.
