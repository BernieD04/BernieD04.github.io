Stem Cell Derived Neurons on an HD-MEA

2026 - present Cellular Neuroscience Imaging Lab Active

Culturing neurons from stem cells and recording their activity on a high-density microelectrode array. This one is just beginning.

The preparation is neurons differentiated from stem cells. The recording side is a 3Brain CorePlate 1W, a single-well high-density microelectrode array that samples extracellular activity at many points across a culture at once.

Both halves are still being set up. There is nothing to report yet, and the current work is building the recording and analysis workflow rather than producing a result.

The signal problem is close to the EMG project. Sorting spikes out of a dense extracellular recording is the same class of problem as decomposing motor unit spike trains out of surface EMG, which is what I am implementing there. What differs is the preparation: here the neurons are differentiated and the network grown before anything is recorded, so the biology is part of the experiment rather than something taken as given.
